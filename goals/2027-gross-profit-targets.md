| Business line | 2027 GP growth target |
|---|---|
| E-commerce | **+$1,000,000** |
| Service | **+$300,000** |

Growth is measured against the 2026 baseline. The Marketing Analytics Specialist owns the
baseline and the single number reported each month. No agent reports its own GP figure to
the department; each one gets its number from Analytics.

## Seasonality

Pool demand peaks from spring through summer. Anything that has to be working by peak season
(tracking, pricing rules, the PPC account structure, Local Services Ads, the Amazon catalog)
should be live by **March 2027**. A role that goes live in June misses most of the year.

## Rules for counting

1. **Count once.** The department total is real gross profit from the books. Role contributions
   are attributed by Analytics and can overlap. Attributed contributions are for steering, not
   for adding up.
2. **Gross profit, not revenue.** GP = net sales − COGS − shipping cost − marketplace and
   payment fees − returns. Ad spend is reported next to GP as "GP after ad spend" so that paid
   channels can't buy revenue at a loss.
3. **Monthly pacing.** Analytics breaks each annual target into a monthly curve that follows the
   season, so the department knows in May (not December) whether it is off track.
