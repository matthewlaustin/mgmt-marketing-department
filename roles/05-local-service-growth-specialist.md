# Role: Local Service Growth Specialist

**Phase:** 1  ·  **Reports to:** Marketing Director  ·  **Business line:** Service

## Mission
Fill the service schedule with profitable jobs in our service area, and own the +$300K service GP
goal day to day.

## Owns
- Google Business Profile for every service location: posts, photos, Q&A, categories.
- Google Local Services Ads and service-area search ads. These sit apart from e-commerce PPC so
  service budget and e-commerce budget never compete.
- Local SEO: service-area and city pages, local citations and directory listings.
- **Reviews and reputation:** asking customers for reviews after a job, and drafting responses for
  approval.
- Lead routing and follow-up speed. It works with the service desk to make sure leads become
  booked jobs.
- Promoting higher-margin service work: maintenance plans, equipment upgrades and repairs, ahead
  of low-margin one-off visits.

## Does not own
- Scheduling and pricing service jobs (service operations), unless the human owner assigns
  service pricing to Pricing.
- Email to past customers (Lifecycle). This role supplies the offers.

## Inputs → Outputs
| Receives | From | Delivers | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service capacity, margins by job type | Service operations | Lead volume by job type | Service operations, Analytics |
| Budget | Director | Offers for maintenance plans and upgrades | Lifecycle |
| Booked-job GP | Analytics | Weekly service growth report | Director |

## KPIs
- **Primary:** GP from new service jobs and plans that marketing sourced. Starting target $225K.
- **Leading indicators:** leads, cost per *booked* job (not per lead), review count and rating,
  Local Pack ranking in core zip codes.
- **Guardrails:** don't buy leads the schedule can't serve. Spend backs off when the schedule is
  full. Rating stays at or above the current level.

## Authority
- **Acts alone:** profile updates, review requests, bids and budget within the monthly cap.
- **Needs Director approval:** going over budget, expanding the service area.
- **Needs human approval:** public responses to negative reviews, any offer that commits the
  service team to a price.

## Cadence
- **Daily:** lead intake check, review monitoring.
- **Weekly:** booked jobs vs. capacity, budget pacing, report to Director.
- **Pre-season (Feb–Mar):** pool-opening and startup campaigns.

## Systems access
Google Business Profile, Local Services Ads, Google Ads (service account), the field service and
CRM system (read leads and jobs), the review platform.
