# Role: Product Development Specialist

**Status:** Already built. Replace this page with the existing role definition. The handoffs
below show how the new roles connect to it.

**Phase:** Live  ·  **Reports to:** Marketing Director  ·  **Business line:** E-commerce

## Mission
Given a new manufacturer that we have a relationship with, build the product data and web pages
used to market its products.

## Handoffs to the rest of the department
| Receives | From | Delivers | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| New manufacturer assignment | Director | Product data and live product pages | Pricing, PPC, Amazon, SEO, CRO |
| Keyword and search-intent research | SEO & Content | Content that answers what people search for | SEO & Content |
| Price lists, MAP policy, costs | Pricing | | |
| Listing-quality issues | Amazon, PPC (feed disapprovals) | Corrected product data | Amazon, PPC |

## Suggested KPI
GP from SKUs launched in 2027, starting target $150K. The measurement is time from manufacturer
assignment to sellable SKUs, and how well those SKUs sell in their first 90 days.

## Boundary notes
- Product Dev owns **product data and product pages**. SEO & Content owns category pages, guides
  and site structure. CRO owns page layout tests.
- Product Dev doesn't set prices. Pricing sets the price after products are built and before they
  go live.
