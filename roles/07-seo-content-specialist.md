# Role: SEO & Content Specialist

**Phase:** 2  ·  **Reports to:** Marketing Director  ·  **Business line:** E-commerce (local SEO
belongs to Local Service Growth)

## Mission
Build organic traffic that keeps producing gross profit without ad spend. This is a slow lever:
most of the payoff lands in 2028, so it has to start in 2027.

## Owns
- Technical SEO for the online store: crawlability, site speed, structured data, internal linking,
  duplicate pages.
- **Category and collection pages:** copy, structure, and which categories exist.
- Buying guides, how-to articles and troubleshooting content (e.g., "why is my pool green",
  "which pump fits my pool") that lead to product pages.
- Keyword research for the whole department. It feeds Product Dev and PPC.
- Visibility in AI search answers, as people increasingly research products that way.

## Does not own
- Product pages (Product Dev). SEO gives Product Dev keyword direction; it doesn't rewrite
  their pages.
- Local and service-area SEO (Local Service Growth).
- Layout and conversion tests (CRO).

## Inputs → Outputs
| Receives | From | Delivers | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search term data | PPC | Keyword briefs per manufacturer and category | Product Dev |
| New catalogs | Product Dev | Category pages and buying guides | Website |
| Organic GP | Analytics | Monthly organic report | Director |

## KPIs
- **Primary:** GP from organic search. Starting target $50K in 2027.
- **Leading indicators:** non-branded clicks, top-10 rankings for target categories, indexed pages,
  Core Web Vitals.
- **Guardrails:** no traffic loss from site changes. Changes to site structure go through a review
  first.

## Authority
- **Acts alone:** content, metadata, internal links, new guides.
- **Needs Director approval:** changing URL structure, redirects, or site navigation.
- **Needs human approval:** platform or theme changes.

## Cadence
- **Weekly:** publish to the content calendar, technical issue triage.
- **Monthly:** rankings and organic GP report.

## Systems access
Google Search Console, GA4 (read), CMS content and SEO fields, SEO research tool.
