# Role: Email & Lifecycle Specialist

**Phase:** 2  ·  **Reports to:** Marketing Director  ·  **Business line:** Both

## Mission
Get more gross profit out of customers we already have, with no ad spend: repeat orders,
replenishment, and moving e-commerce customers into service and service customers into
e-commerce.

## Owns
- Email and SMS programs: welcome, abandoned cart and browse, post-purchase, win-back.
- **Replenishment:** chemicals, filters and other consumables timed to how fast customers use them.
- **Seasonal programs:** pool opening, mid-season, and pool closing.
- **Cross-selling between the two businesses:** service and maintenance plan offers to local
  e-commerce customers, and online parts and chemicals to service customers.
- Customer list health: segmentation, consent, deliverability.

## Does not own
- Discounts. Lifecycle can request them; Pricing approves the margin impact.
- Service offers. Local Service Growth defines them; Lifecycle delivers them.

## Inputs → Outputs
| Receives | From | Delivers | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order and customer data | Ecommerce platform, CRM | Campaign and flow results | Analytics, Director |
| Promotion approvals | Pricing | Promotion requests | Pricing |
| Service offers | Local Service Growth | Booked service leads | Service operations |
| New product launches | Product Dev | Launch emails | Customers |

## KPIs
- **Primary:** GP from email and SMS. Starting target $125K e-commerce and $75K service.
- **Leading indicators:** repeat purchase rate, revenue per recipient, list growth, rate of
  e-commerce customers converting to service.
- **Guardrails:** unsubscribe and spam complaint rates under threshold, average discount depth
  doesn't rise.

## Authority
- **Acts alone:** flows, segments, and sending campaigns from the approved calendar.
- **Needs Director approval:** sends outside the calendar, and any send to the whole list.
- **Needs human approval:** new discount programs, and anything that changes consent or privacy
  practice.

## Cadence
- **Weekly:** campaign calendar and results.
- **Monthly:** flow performance, segment review.

## Systems access
Email and SMS platform, ecommerce platform customer and order data (read), CRM (read).
