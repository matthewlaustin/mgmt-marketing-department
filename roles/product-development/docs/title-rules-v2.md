# Pentair title rules v2 (from Matthew's training, 2026-10-25)

These rules replace `pentair-title-rules.md` wherever the two disagree. That file still holds
the mechanics: reading the pages, trademark spellings, Z suffix, and the clean-up list of units,
abbreviations and typos. Use only words Pentair prints. Never invent a word.

## Format
`{Brand} {MPN} - {Title}`
- **Brand:** Pentair, Sta-Rite or Pleatco. A part or accessory takes the brand of the product line
  it belongs to, so Sta-Rite lines (circle-marked in the catalog directory, e.g. System:3®,
  SuperMax®, IntelliProXF®) give Sta-Rite. A part number listed under both a Pentair and a
  Sta-Rite line is `Pentair/Sta-Rite`. Pleatco cartridges, including Pentair-numbered cartridge
  packs, are Pleatco.
- **Discontinued or not for the USA:** no title. Leave these out.

## Parts
The title is the part's own line-item description, cleaned up. Nothing else.
- Don't add the product line, the page heading, the band, a generic noun ("Pump", "Cleaner") or a
  fit list. Examples: "Diffuser", "Lid", "Shaft Seal", "Center Diverter", "Rear Cover Kit",
  "Power Supply", "Seal/Gasket Kit".
- Keep a trademark only when the line item itself prints it: "QuikSkim Ultra Silt Sock".
- Descriptors printed after a comma go in front of the noun:
  - "Tank lid, 24 sq. ft. filter" → "24 sq ft Filter Tank Lid"
  - "Plaster ring, for gunite niche, spa" → "Spa Gunite Niche Plaster Ring"
  - "Compressor, Scroll ZP61" → "Scroll Compressor ZP61"
- Drop text in brackets and fit notes such as "(Model 90 only)" or "FG units built before 1/6/21".
- Keep a "for …" phrase printed without a comma: "Battery for Indoor Control Panel and Outdoor
  Control Panel".
- **Model names go in front of the noun:** "Model 250 NG to Propane Conversion Kit",
  "S7M120/S7M400 Modular Media Conversion Kit", "194R/195R Replacement Brushes".
- **Model charts** (one row per pump model, one column per part): use the model, then the column
  header's noun: "EQK1000 Motor".
- **Disambiguate only within one parts list.** If two rows in the same list would get the same
  title, add the printed word that tells them apart (size, colour, model), in front of the noun.
  Duplicate titles across different parts lists are fine.
- Pack counts lead: "Set of 4 Ultra Replacement Cartridges".

## Accessories
Same as parts.

## Whole goods
- The product name as Pentair prints it: family, model, noun ("IntelliFlo® VS+SVRS Pump").
- Add every spec column that varies in the series' ordering table: voltage, watts, cord length,
  HP, gas type, BTU, ASME, HD and so on. Example: "IntelliBrite® Architectural Series Color 120V
  28W 100 ft Cord Pool Light".
- Heater sizes are written "400K BTU": "MasterTemp® 400K BTU Natural Gas Heater".
- No marketing words. Always remove: High Performance, Variable Speed, Self-Priming, Commercial,
  DOE Compliant, Heavy Duty. Remove other adjectives that only promote the product. ("Low Profile"
  is a valve line name, not marketing; see below.)
- Drop fit and application tails ("for C, CC and D Series Pumps").

## Pleatco cartridges
Keep the line, sq ft and dimensions: "Advanced Filter Cartridge 40 sq ft 7 in x 14-1/16 in".

## Writing
- Title Case. Units are standard lower-case abbreviations: "24 sq ft", "2 in", "100 ft", "120V",
  "28W", "1-1/2 HP".

## Settled by the 40-SKU check [2026-10-25]
Matthew approved these calls:
- **Abbreviations are spelled out**, even if no nearby page spells them: Assy → Assembly,
  Subassy → Subassembly, Hx Hd → Hex Head, MPV → Multiport Valve. Leave product codes and
  trademarks alone.
- **Packaging words never go in a title**: display box, bubble pack, bulk, carded, and so on.
  Two numbers that differ only by packaging share a title.
- **When pages describe one SKU differently**, use the wording most pages print. On a tie, use the
  product's own page or parts list.
  - 470161: Torx (2 pages) wins over Square Drive (1 page).
  - 475002: the Max-E-Therm® page's "Air Intake Kit".
  - RCS1: the parts lists' "Wall Mount Single Gang Plate Spa Jets Switch".
  - 154512Z: its title page's "6-1/2 in Across Flats Wrench".
- **Descriptor placement:** a descriptor printed after a comma goes immediately before the noun
  phrase, and colour stays closest to the noun:
  - "Small PVC niche, concrete" → "Small PVC Concrete Niche"
  - "Strainer Pot, Black" → "Black Strainer Pot"
  - "Bottom Cover - Gray" → "Gray Bottom Cover"
  Noun-first text is put in natural order even without a comma: "Tube air relief TR50/60" →
  "TR50/60 Air Relief Tube".
- **A whole good with no noun of its own** takes the noun from the page heading:
  "G4VHP Complete White Cleaning Head with Riser".
- **A whole good's fit tail** is kept only when it is the only printed difference from a sibling
  in the same table: "1-1/2 in Multiport Valve Kit for Triton & Quad D.E. Filters".
- **Voltage** is written as "V": "525-600V", "208-230V". HP is written as printed ("3.0 HP").
- **Pleatco:**
  - a cartridge with no printed sq ft keeps its dimensions; if a sibling has the same dimensions,
    add the printed ", Upgrade for X";
  - D.E. grid sets follow the cartridge pattern: "Universal DE Grid Set 48 sq ft 11 in x 24 in".
- **Within one parts list** includes several bands on one page: "Title-22 Controller Circuit Board".
- **Pack counts:** "Set of N" in front, noun in the plural: "Set of 5 … Black Trim Rings".
- **Typos** are corrected when the right spelling is printed on the same page. A lower-case MPN
  suffix ("410020z") is written upper case.
- **Repeated words:** "Kit-THS3461, single tank manifold, with butterfly valve kit" →
  "THS3461 Single Tank Manifold with Butterfly Valve Kit".

## Settled by the pilot [2026-10-25]
- **Ordering-table bundles** (IntelliCenter® systems): use Pentair's description wording. Add an
  "Includes" item only when it is what tells two rows apart. Example: "IntelliCenter® System with
  i5P Personality Kit and IntelliChlor® ICPLUS40 Cell". Leave out columns that follow from the kit
  (the SCG transformer, valves).
- **Whole-good fit tails are dropped**, even when Pentair's name includes them:
  "Kreepy Krauly® 'Lil Shark™ Automatic Pool Cleaner".
- **Trademarks always carry their registered mark**, even where a row prints the name bare:
  "IntelliCenter® White Faceplate Indoor Control Panel". This applies to parts and accessories
  too, wherever the trademark name appears.
- **Ambiguous abbreviations:** btr. → Buttress, ASY → Assembly. If an abbreviation still can't
  be read, mark it unsure.
- **356210S** is "Mechanical Seal". The majority wording rule stands, and the bracketed
  "(ozone/salt resistant)" is dropped.

## Interim calls during the full run [to confirm with Matthew on the final page]
- **Pleatco differentiators** other than "Upgrade for" go at the end after a comma, like
  ", Upgrade for X": ", Short Thread", ", 1-1/2 in SAE Bottom Hole".
- **Pleatco packs:** use the sq ft printed on the pack's row (the pack total when that is what is
  printed): "Set of 4 Advanced Filter Cartridges 224 sq ft 7 in x 17-3/8 in".
- **Pleatco line names** (Ultra, Advanced) carry no ™, as in Matthew's approved examples.
- **", Upgrade for X"** names only the first Pleatco number printed in that cell.
- **Conflicting dimensions between Pleatco pages:** use the measurement page and mark unsure.
- **Page ties:** the product line's own page or parts list wins over a shared accessories table
  (p19 or the shared heater accessories table). Matthew approved this for RCS1 and 475002.
- **Whole-good spec columns:** add the columns a buyer picks a model by (size or area, HP,
  voltage, watts, cord length, gas type, colour, ASME/HD) when the model name doesn't already carry
  them. Leave out performance and shipping columns (flow, turnover, WEF, amps, weight, chlorine
  output), as in the approved IntelliFlo3® title. Don't repeat a size the model name already
  states: "Clean & Clear® CC 100 Filter", not "CC 100 100 sq ft".
- **Ultra cartridges** carry no sq ft, even where another page prints one (approved ULTRA-B10).
- **Pleatco line names stay** for every Pleatco product: PureStart, Filter Wash™, Advanced, Ultra.
- **Grid sets** follow the Pleatco grid pattern even when parts lists word them differently.
- **Model lists** are written with slashes and no spaces: "G4/G4V/G4VHP", "TR100C/TR140C".
- **A whole good whose row leaves out a family word** that its heading and siblings print stays as
  printed; mark it unsure.
- **Orderability:** a printed number that the page's own numbering key says needs an option letter
  (Stark™ systems, AQ vessels) is titled normally and marked unsure. One question for Matthew.
- **A ", for …" phrase after a comma:**
  - If it names a use or installation type, it moves in front without "for", as in Matthew's
    plaster ring example: "Regenerative D.E. Filter Quick Disconnect Kit".
  - If it names other products the part fits, or a condition ("installations without valve"), it
    is dropped.
- **"Low Profile"** is the name of Pentair's in-floor valve line (the counterpart of Top Feed), not
  a marketing word. Keep it wherever it names that line: "6 Port Low Profile Retro Kit",
  "WAVE 6 Port Low Profile 2 in T-Valve". I put it on Matthew's marketing list by mistake; to confirm.
- **Matthew's earlier decisions still hold.** data/work/pentair/owner_decisions.json lists them.
  For example, 301300 is "Waterco 301300 - MultiCyclone 16 Pro Pre Filter".
- **SS** is written out as "Stainless Steel" (abbreviation rule), except where it is part of a
  name ("Stark™ SS Series"). "Only" stays when it says what is not included ("Cover Only").
- **Colour with a named product** (open, for Matthew): models and trademark names lead, and the
  colour goes right before the item's own noun ("G4V White Internal"). Where the name runs into
  the noun ("BlueSquare Retro Ratcheting Head Multi Orifice Cap"), the colour leads the whole
  name, as in the 40-SKU check's 220065: "White BlueSquare Retro Ratcheting Head Multi Orifice Cap".
  Keep one page consistent.
- **A model printed after "for"** in a part's line item goes in front of the noun, like Matthew's
  "194R/195R Replacement Brushes" (printed "For No. 194 and No. 195"):
  "100 sq. ft. Inner Replacement Module for S7M120" → "S7M120 100 sq ft Inner Replacement Module".
- **Model column on whole goods and accessories:** when a row has both a description and a Model
  number, the model goes in front as printed. A bare column value stays bare ("194R/195R …",
  "127 Tube Thermometer"). "No. 801" or "Model 250" are written that way only where the row prints it.
- **Siblings keep one pattern.** If the majority-wording rule would word sibling SKUs in one table
  differently (e.g. the three Seal Quick Kits, or the union kits on one list), use the wording of
  the table they share for all of them.
- **"Super-Duty"** is treated as a promotional word like Heavy Duty (to confirm).
- **Competitor models in basket titles stay.** Matthew decided this in round 1:
  "Hayward® SP 1070 Replacement Basket".
- **"Commercial" as part of a line name** (Commercial IntelliChlor®, Commercial IntelliChem®,
  Commercial PowerMax®): Matthew ticked Commercial as marketing, so it stays out for now. These SKUs
  are marked unsure and will be shown to him with examples, the same issue as "Low Profile".
- **Matthew's own training answers beat the general rules.** Parts-Paks™ seal/gasket kits are
  "Seal/Gasket Kit" (his PP1010 answer), even though every kit on p243 then shares that title.
  Mark them unsure so he sees the effect.
- **Pages for items not sold in the USA** (50 Hz pages, international pages) don't count toward the
  majority wording.
- **"5PK" kits:** "Set of 5 IC Cross Guards" (drop "Kit").
- **One page, one style:** a later title on a page follows that page's already-final siblings
  ("Plus40 and Plus60 Spacer Cell" → "LT15, LT25 and Plus30 Spacer Cell").
- **Skimmer lids that print only fit brands:** Matthew approved L1 as "9-7/8 in White Skimmer Lid
  with Thermometer", with no brands. Add the fit brands only where two lids in one list would
  otherwise share a title (L1RW vs L2R).
- **Tie between a product's own ordering page and its own parts list:** use the ordering page.
- **HiFlow™** is the canonical spelling (consolidate rewrites "Hi-Flow™"). Kept fit tails carry
  their trademarks' marks too ("for Triton® & Quad D.E.® Filters").
- **"No X" descriptors** ("no plumbing", "no fittings") go at the end after a comma:
  "2 in HiFlow™ Valve, No Plumbing".
- **No printed description at all** (only a diagram label or a bare number): record a gap with
  `"title": "", "gap": true`, and say why. Never title a SKU from a picture or a guess (CLAUDE.md).
- **Don't add "with"** (or any other word) that Pentair doesn't print. A comma list of included
  items that can't move in front stays after the noun, after a comma, in printed order:
  "…, Spacer/Locknut".
- **Word order in front of the noun** (settles "colour closest to the noun"): model or name, then
  size and other specs, then colour, then material, then the noun. Examples: "White Plastic Mender
  Nut" (not "Plastic White"), "9-7/8 in White Skimmer Lid", "Bermuda™ 2 in Slip Ports White PVC
  Gunite Skimmer". The final sweep applies this to batches finalized earlier.
- **Tie between two parts lists of one line:** use the wording that prints more of the item
  (colour, size), as the b45 adjudication did. Mark it unsure only when the two pages really
  contradict each other (a different noun, colour or size), e.g. EC110 "Turbine Shaft" vs
  "Turbine Cover".
- **Spelling variants** (Adaptor/Adapter, Grey/Gray within one SKU's pages): use the catalog's usual
  spelling ("Adapter") unless the brand prints a name that way.
- **"Bulk pack N" and "(Set of N)":** the number is a pack count, written "Set of N" in front with a
  plural noun. The word "bulk" is dropped.
- **AC voltage:** "24VAC" is written "24V AC".
- **Open round-2 questions** (85018000 beige/tan, 523434, 18201-0300/263053 brand, 521057Z,
  160448/188616, ULTRA-A18, 192115Z, 98211400Z, 150085Z, 470161, 9104800Z) keep their SKUs unsure
  until Matthew answers.
- **A date footnote that is the only difference** between two parts in one list goes after the
  noun in short form (Matthew: extra words only to tell parts apart): "Drain Plug O-Ring for
  Filters Made After 5/21/05".
- **One SKU printed twice on one page with different wording:** use the row in the SKU's own table
  (its item number in the parts list) and mark it unsure.
- **Identical rows in one parts list:** use words another page prints for the same SKU to tell
  them apart ("#2-231 O-Ring" from p453). If no page does, they share the title and stay unsure.
- **A bracketed date that is the only difference** between sibling SKUs stays, in short form,
  after the noun, like the footnote-date call: "Cord-Ring for Filters Made Before 3/97".
- **Codes vs abbreviations:** product and line codes stay as printed (IT/ET, LC/PC, ICPLUS40).
  Abbreviated ordinary words are spelled out (OCP → Outdoor Control Panel, where the page prints
  it).
- **A trademark the catalog never prints with a mark** (ScreenLogic, QuikSkim, AutoFill) stays bare.
  Marks are added only where Pentair prints them somewhere.
- **Brackets that hold a spec of the item itself** (amps, conductor count, size: "(3 Amp)",
  "(6-Conductor)") stay, without brackets, in front of the noun: "3 Amp Circuit Breaker". Brackets
  holding notes, fits or contents are still dropped.
- **"Aux"** is spelled out as "Auxiliary", under the abbreviation rule ("8 Auxiliary …").
- **Printed change dates** that are the only difference stay in printed words at the end:
  "…, Before Change Date 2011".
- **A parts page carrying the STA-RITE logo** counts as a Sta-Rite line even when the directory
  doesn't list the family (D Series™, Posi-Clear™ RP, Dynamo®).
- **A printed list of sizes** is written with slashes: "150/200 sq ft Filter Lid".
- **A model that differs by parts list** (TR100/140 on one, TR100C/TR140C on another) is fit
  information. Leave it out unless it is needed to tell rows apart within one list. If it is
  needed, use the model printed on the list where the clash happens.
- Date footnotes use the short form from the footnote-date call: "… for Filters Made After 11/1/99"
  (not "Filters Manufactured After 11-1-99").
- **NG and LP stay as printed.** Matthew approved "Model 250 NG to Propane Conversion Kit".
- **Ties across two lines' parts lists:** use the wording of the list whose siblings the SKU sits
  with in this batch (one page, one style), and mark it unsure only if the nouns contradict.
- **A drawing dimension that is the only difference** ("(dim. D = 19-7/8 in.)") goes after the noun
  after a comma: "Piping Assembly, Dimension D 19-7/8 in". Mark it unsure.
- **Footnote or asterisk marks with no legend on the page** are dropped; the SKU stays. Say so in
  `why`. Only a mark the page defines as discontinued removes a SKU.
- **Board or drawing numbers inside a description** ("#11111") are dropped.
- **"(KIT)" in brackets** says what is sold, so it stays as "Kit" after the noun: "Burner Kit".
- **Bracketed colour** is dropped unless it is the only difference (then it goes in front). A
  bracketed gas type is a spec and goes in front; if that repeats a word, put it after a comma
  instead ("Gas and Air Orifices, Natural Gas") and mark it unsure.
- **Trademark spelling:** use the catalog's usual spelling and mark from its running text
  ("Titan-Tough™"), as with HiFlow™.
- **Abbreviations no page spells out** ("HP" in "Control/HP Panel") stay as printed, unsure.
- **"200 Models" / "333 Model"** become "Model 200" / "Model 333" in front, as in the b10 kits.
- **A bare count in brackets** ("(4)", "(Qty. 5)") is a pack count: "Set of 4".
- **A non-spec bracket that is the only difference** goes after the noun after a comma:
  "Side Panel, Service Panel".
- **A bracketed fit or phase that is the only difference** ("(For ZP70 Compressor)", "(3-PHASE
  MODELS)") goes in front: "ZP70 Hybrid Compressor Harness", "3-Phase Scroll Compressor ZP83".
- **Compressors** follow Matthew's "Scroll Compressor ZP61" example: model after the noun.
- **When majority wording would give two SKUs on one list the same title,** use the page wording
  that keeps them apart, unsure.
- **A bracket mixing models and a date** that is the only difference: models in front, date after
  the noun ("Model 70 Titanium Heat Exchanger for Units Built After Jan 2017"); an "all models
  except …" list is left out.
- **Bracketed dates on heat pump lists:** don't add a noun the row doesn't print ("Units"). If the
  row names what was made ("Heaters", "Filters"), use the short footnote-date form; otherwise keep
  the printed words after a comma: "Heat Pump Contactor, Prior to 12/2014".
- **Cross-line ties never break a settled pattern:** where Matthew approved a pattern ("Scroll
  Compressor ZP61"), use the page wording that keeps it, even across lines.
- **Cross-line tie vs siblings' pattern:** when the SKU sits with the same siblings in several
  lines' tables and the siblings settle one wording (by majority or a fuller page), use that
  wording, so the tables share one pattern.
- **Counts of a kit's contents** ("(4 each)", O-ring kits "(6)/(8)/(10)") are dropped; "Set of N" is
  only for the number of items sold. Mark unsure where it isn't clear which it is.
- **Matthew's approved sibling decides the page:** where he approved a sibling's title from one
  table (R201536 "194R/195R Replacement Brushes", from p426), the siblings follow that table.
- **"(Xn)" / "(n req.)" on an equipment parts list** usually count how many one unit uses, not how
  many are sold. Drop them. Only if another page prints the same SKU as a pack ("Set of N", "N per
  card", "bulk pack") is it a pack (title it so). Don't mark the dropped count unsure: the review
  page asks Matthew once for the whole group. The bare-count call above applies only to accessory
  and ordering rows.
- **Whole-good specs from pentair.com:** when the catalog row doesn't print a spec the series'
  ordering table varies by, Pentair's own cached product page may supply it (cite it in `why`).
- **Per-unit counts don't split the page count:** "Flat Washer (Qty. 6)" and "Flat Washer" count as
  the same wording for the majority.
- **A row on a shared list that names its own line** ("WhisperFloXF VS Wet End Assembly") takes
  that line's brand; rows that name no line count for every line the list covers.
- **Old "(parts are Pentair)" notes in batch context are out of date.** Some batch contexts carry
  this v1 note next to a STA-RITE logo. Ignore it: the brand rules above decide (a Sta-Rite-logo
  parts page is a Sta-Rite line; a part on both Pentair and Sta-Rite lines is Pentair/Sta-Rite).
- **Model codes containing fractions** ("1/2F, 3/4A") keep the printed commas, since slashes would be
  unreadable; one question for Matthew, marked unsure.
- **Pump motor abbreviations:** 1SP → "Single Speed" (p450 band), 1PH/3PH → "1-Phase"/"3-Phase" (as
  the motor pages print). Use one form for all pump motors.
