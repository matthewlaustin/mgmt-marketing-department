# Pentair title rules v2 (from Matthew's training, 2026-10-25)

These rules replace `pentair-title-rules.md` wherever the two disagree. That file still holds
the mechanics: reading the pages, trademark spellings, Z suffix, and the clean-up list of units,
abbreviations and typos. Use only words Pentair prints. Never invent a word.

## Format
`{Brand} {MPN} - {Title}`
- **Brand:** Pentair, Sta-Rite or Pleatco. A part or accessory takes the brand of the product line
  it belongs to, so Sta-Rite lines (circle-marked in the catalog directory, e.g. System:3®,
  SuperMax®, IntelliProXF®) give Sta-Rite. A part number listed under both a Pentair and a
  Sta-Rite line is `Pentair/Sta-Rite`. Pleatco cartridges, including Pentair-numbered cartridge
  packs, are Pleatco.
- **Discontinued or not for the USA:** no title. Leave these out.

## Parts
The title is the part's own line-item description, cleaned up. Nothing else.
- Don't add the product line, the page heading, the band, a generic noun ("Pump", "Cleaner") or a
  fit list. Examples: "Diffuser", "Lid", "Shaft Seal", "Center Diverter", "Rear Cover Kit",
  "Power Supply", "Seal/Gasket Kit".
- Keep a trademark only when the line item itself prints it: "QuikSkim Ultra Silt Sock".
- Descriptors printed after a comma go in front of the noun:
  - "Tank lid, 24 sq. ft. filter" → "24 sq ft Filter Tank Lid"
  - "Plaster ring, for gunite niche, spa" → "Spa Gunite Niche Plaster Ring"
  - "Compressor, Scroll ZP61" → "Scroll Compressor ZP61"
- Drop text in brackets and fit notes such as "(Model 90 only)" or "FG units built before 1/6/21".
- Keep a "for …" phrase printed without a comma: "Battery for Indoor Control Panel and Outdoor
  Control Panel".
- **Model names go in front of the noun:** "Model 250 NG to Propane Conversion Kit",
  "S7M120/S7M400 Modular Media Conversion Kit", "194R/195R Replacement Brushes".
- **Model charts** (one row per pump model, one column per part): use the model, then the column
  header's noun: "EQK1000 Motor".
- **Disambiguate only within one parts list.** If two rows in the same list would get the same
  title, add the printed word that tells them apart (size, colour, model), in front of the noun.
  Duplicate titles across different parts lists are fine.
- Pack counts lead: "Set of 4 Ultra Replacement Cartridges".

## Accessories
Same as parts.

## Whole goods
- The product name as Pentair prints it: family, model, noun ("IntelliFlo® VS+SVRS Pump").
- Add every spec column that varies in the series' ordering table: voltage, watts, cord length,
  HP, gas type, BTU, ASME, HD and so on. Example: "IntelliBrite® Architectural Series Color 120V
  28W 100 ft Cord Pool Light".
- Heater sizes are written "400K BTU": "MasterTemp® 400K BTU Natural Gas Heater".
- No marketing words. Always remove: High Performance, Variable Speed, Self-Priming, Commercial,
  DOE Compliant, Low Profile, Heavy Duty. Remove other adjectives that only promote the product.
- Drop fit and application tails ("for C, CC and D Series Pumps").

## Pleatco cartridges
Keep the line, sq ft and dimensions: "Advanced Filter Cartridge 40 sq ft 7 in x 14-1/16 in".

## Writing
- Title Case. Units are standard lower-case abbreviations: "24 sq ft", "2 in", "100 ft", "120V",
  "28W", "1-1/2 HP".

## Settled by the 40-SKU check [2026-10-25]
Matthew approved these calls:
- **Abbreviations are spelled out**, even if no nearby page spells them: Assy → Assembly,
  Subassy → Subassembly, Hx Hd → Hex Head, MPV → Multiport Valve. Leave product codes and
  trademarks alone.
- **Packaging words never go in a title**: display box, bubble pack, bulk, carded, and so on.
  Two numbers that differ only by packaging share a title.
- **When pages describe one SKU differently**, use the wording most pages print. On a tie, use the
  product's own page or parts list.
  - 470161: Torx (2 pages) wins over Square Drive (1 page).
  - 475002: the Max-E-Therm® page's "Air Intake Kit".
  - RCS1: the parts lists' "Wall Mount Single Gang Plate Spa Jets Switch".
  - 154512Z: its title page's "6-1/2 in Across Flats Wrench".
- **Descriptor placement:** a descriptor printed after a comma goes immediately before the noun
  phrase, and colour stays closest to the noun:
  - "Small PVC niche, concrete" → "Small PVC Concrete Niche"
  - "Strainer Pot, Black" → "Black Strainer Pot"
  - "Bottom Cover - Gray" → "Gray Bottom Cover"
  Noun-first text is put in natural order even without a comma: "Tube air relief TR50/60" →
  "TR50/60 Air Relief Tube".
- **A whole good with no noun of its own** takes the noun from the page heading:
  "G4VHP Complete White Cleaning Head with Riser".
- **A whole good's fit tail** is kept only when it is the only printed difference from a sibling
  in the same table: "1-1/2 in Multiport Valve Kit for Triton & Quad D.E. Filters".
- **Voltage** is written as "V": "525-600V", "208-230V". HP is written as printed ("3.0 HP").
- **Pleatco:**
  - a cartridge with no printed sq ft keeps its dimensions; if a sibling has the same dimensions,
    add the printed ", Upgrade for X";
  - D.E. grid sets follow the cartridge pattern: "Universal DE Grid Set 48 sq ft 11 in x 24 in".
- **Within one parts list** includes several bands on one page: "Title-22 Controller Circuit Board".
- **Pack counts:** "Set of N" in front, noun in the plural: "Set of 5 … Black Trim Rings".
- **Typos** are corrected when the right spelling is printed on the same page. A lower-case MPN
  suffix ("410020z") is written upper case.
- **Repeated words:** "Kit-THS3461, single tank manifold, with butterfly valve kit" →
  "THS3461 Single Tank Manifold with Butterfly Valve Kit".

## Settled by the pilot [2026-10-25]
- **Ordering-table bundles** (IntelliCenter® systems): use Pentair's description wording. Add an
  "Includes" item only when it is what tells two rows apart. Example: "IntelliCenter® System with
  i5P Personality Kit and IntelliChlor® ICPLUS40 Cell". Leave out columns that follow from the kit
  (the SCG transformer, valves).
- **Whole-good fit tails are dropped**, even when Pentair's name includes them:
  "Kreepy Krauly® 'Lil Shark™ Automatic Pool Cleaner".
- **Trademarks always carry their registered mark**, even where a row prints the name bare:
  "IntelliCenter® White Faceplate Indoor Control Panel". This applies to parts and accessories
  too, wherever the trademark name appears.
- **Ambiguous abbreviations:** btr. → Buttress, ASY → Assembly. If an abbreviation still can't
  be read, mark it unsure.
- **356210S** is "Mechanical Seal". The majority wording rule stands, and the bracketed
  "(ozone/salt resistant)" is dropped.

## Interim calls during the full run [to confirm with Matthew on the final page]
- **Pleatco differentiators** other than "Upgrade for" go at the end after a comma, like
  ", Upgrade for X": ", Short Thread", ", 1-1/2 in SAE Bottom Hole".
- **Pleatco packs:** use the sq ft printed on the pack's row (the pack total when that is what is
  printed): "Set of 4 Advanced Filter Cartridges 224 sq ft 7 in x 17-3/8 in".
- **Pleatco line names** (Ultra, Advanced) carry no ™, as in Matthew's approved examples.
- **", Upgrade for X"** names only the first Pleatco number printed in that cell.
- **Conflicting dimensions between Pleatco pages:** use the measurement page and mark unsure.
- **Page ties:** the product line's own page or parts list wins over a shared accessories table
  (p19 or the shared heater accessories table). Matthew approved this for RCS1 and 475002.
- **Whole-good spec columns:** add the columns a buyer picks a model by (size or area, HP,
  voltage, watts, cord length, gas type, colour, ASME/HD) when the model name doesn't already carry
  them. Leave out performance and shipping columns (flow, turnover, WEF, amps, weight, chlorine
  output), as in the approved IntelliFlo3® title. Don't repeat a size the model name already
  states: "Clean & Clear® CC 100 Filter", not "CC 100 100 sq ft".
- **Ultra cartridges** carry no sq ft, even where another page prints one (approved ULTRA-B10).
- **Pleatco line names stay** for every Pleatco product: PureStart, Filter Wash™, Advanced, Ultra.
- **Grid sets** follow the Pleatco grid pattern even when parts lists word them differently.
- **Model lists** are written with slashes and no spaces: "G4/G4V/G4VHP", "TR100C/TR140C".
- **A whole good whose row leaves out a family word** that its heading and siblings print stays as
  printed; mark it unsure.
- **Orderability:** a printed number that the page's own numbering key says needs an option letter
  (Stark™ systems, AQ vessels) is titled normally and marked unsure. One question for Matthew.
- **A ", for …" phrase after a comma:**
  - If it names a use or installation type, it moves in front without "for", as in Matthew's
    plaster ring example: "Regenerative D.E. Filter Quick Disconnect Kit".
  - If it names other products the part fits, or a condition ("installations without valve"), it
    is dropped.
- **"Low Profile"** is the name of Pentair's in-floor valve line (the counterpart of Top Feed), not
  a marketing word. Keep it wherever it names that line: "6 Port Low Profile Retro Kit",
  "WAVE 6 Port Low Profile 2 in T-Valve". I put it on Matthew's marketing list by mistake; to confirm.
