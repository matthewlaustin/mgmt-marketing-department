Parts diagrams, brochures and where to keep the files

What was wrong with the parts diagrams and how they are rendered now, the brochures and sell sheets found on pentair.com, and the storage question. Three answers to give; every diagram is at the bottom to check.

Figures

What was wrong with the diagrams

  • Most "diagrams" were not diagrams. Any catalog page with a parts row got a render, and 124 of the 299 pages were accessory tables on whole-goods pages (filters, heaters, automation) with no exploded view at all. Those parts now carry no diagram.
  • The crop followed the page text, not the drawing. It kept the coloured side tab, the Proposition 65 box frame with its warning triangles, the black table-header bars and a blank right third, so the drawing came out small and framed by junk.
  • Drawings beside or below a table were missed (the lighting pages, the vacuums, the heat pumps) and 22 table-only continuation pages got whole-page renders.

What changed

  • Each drawing is found from the PDF's own vector lines and embedded artwork, so the crop is the drawing plus its callout numbers and captions. Tables, headings, side tabs and the Prop 65 box are painted out where a scanned drawing runs under them.
  • Rendered at 300 dpi from the vectors and trimmed to the ink, so it is sharp at any size. Pages with several drawings give several images; a "(CONT'D)" page reuses the drawing of the page it continues.
