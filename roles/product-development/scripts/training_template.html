27 real Pentair SKUs, one or two of each kind I name. Each shows the catalog row (tap “Full page” for the whole page), today's title, and my draft under your new rule. Tap “Draft is right”, or type what you would have used. Notes are for anything that belongs in another field.

The rule I'm drafting from

  • The title is what Pentair calls the item, plus its own specs: size, colour, HP, voltage, pack count.
  • Product line, fit lists, model numbers, build dates, competitor brands and use (“for gunite niche”) go in their own fields. Several different parts may share one title.
  • My assumption: a part keeps a plain word for what it goes on (“Pump”, “Cleaner”, “Skimmer”) when Pentair prints it on the page.
